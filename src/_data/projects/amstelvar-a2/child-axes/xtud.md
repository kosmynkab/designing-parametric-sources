## Expected behaviour

XTUD changes horizontal transparency in uppercase diagonal constructions while preserving their intended angles, meeting points, and overall construction where possible.

### Controls

A, M, and V provide diagonal uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTUD is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-diagonal child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
