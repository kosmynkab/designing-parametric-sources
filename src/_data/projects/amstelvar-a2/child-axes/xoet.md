## Expected behaviour

XOET changes the thickness of thick-stroke components in punctuation and
other glyphs while preserving each glyph’s intended construction and
proportion where possible.

### Controls

Exclamation mark, parenthesis, and at sign provide different constructions for
assessing the transformation across punctuation and symbols.

### Relationship to XOPQ

XOET is Amstelvar Avar2’s other-glyph child axis of the parent
[XOPQ](/reference/axes/xopq/) thick-stroke transformation.
