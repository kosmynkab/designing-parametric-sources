## Expected behaviour

XLCS changes the sidebearings of lowercase straight constructions while preserving the drawing and intended spacing relationship with neighbouring forms.

### Controls

l, n, and r provide lowercase straight and shoulder constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XLCS is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-straight child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
