## Expected behaviour

YOLC changes the thickness of thin-stroke components in lowercase glyphs
while preserving their intended construction, proportions, and spacing where
possible.

### Controls

n, o, p, r, and v provide straight, round, descender, shoulder, and diagonal
lowercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YOPQ

YOLC is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase child axis of the parent
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) thin-stroke transformation.
