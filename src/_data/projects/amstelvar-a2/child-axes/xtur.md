## Expected behaviour

XTUR changes horizontal transparency in uppercase round constructions while preserving their intended curve relationships and overall construction where possible.

### Controls

B, C, and O provide different uppercase round constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTUR is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-round child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
