## Expected behaviour

XOFI changes the thickness of thick-stroke components in figures while
preserving their intended construction, proportions, and spacing where
possible.

### Controls

Zero, one, four, and seven provide complementary figure constructions for
assessing the transformation: round, straight-stem, open, and diagonal forms.

### Relationship to XOPQ

XOFI is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent
[XOPQ](/reference/axes/xopq/) thick-stroke transformation.
