## Expected behaviour

YSVA concerns the vertical extent of vertical serifs. The intended
transformation changes their height or depth while preserving their
attachment, proportion, and relationship to the vertical construction they
complete.

> round glyphs - the serifs collapse only to the point of breaking. Example C, G
