## Expected behaviour

XTLD changes horizontal transparency in lowercase diagonal constructions while preserving their intended angle, meeting point, and overall construction where possible.

### Controls

v is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTLD is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-diagonal child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
