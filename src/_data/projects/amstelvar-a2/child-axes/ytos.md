## Expected behaviour

YTOS changes how far round forms and pointed vertices extend beyond their relevant alignment zones while preserving their relationship to the baseline, x-height, cap height, and other vertical measures.

### Controls

O, V, o, v, zero, and four provide uppercase, lowercase, and figure controls for assessing overshoot in round and pointed forms.

### Relationship to YTOS

YTOS is Amstelvar Avar2’s child axis of the parent [YTOS](/reference/axes/ytos/) vertical-overshoot transformation.
