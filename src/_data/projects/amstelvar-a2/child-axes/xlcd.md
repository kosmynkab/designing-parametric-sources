## Expected behaviour

XLCD changes the sidebearings of lowercase diagonal constructions while preserving their intended visual extent and relationship to neighbouring forms.

### Controls

v is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XLCD is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-diagonal child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
