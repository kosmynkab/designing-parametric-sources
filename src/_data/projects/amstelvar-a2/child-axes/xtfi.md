## Expected behaviour

XTFI changes horizontal transparency in figures while preserving their intended construction, proportions, and spacing where possible.

### Controls

Zero, one, four, and seven provide complementary figure constructions for assessing the transformation: round, straight-stem, open, and diagonal forms.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTFI is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
