## Expected behaviour

XTLC changes horizontal transparency in lowercase straight constructions while preserving their intended structure, proportions, and spacing where possible.

### Controls

n and r provide lowercase straight and shoulder constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTLC is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-straight child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
