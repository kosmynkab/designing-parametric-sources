## Expected behaviour

XTUA changes horizontal transparency in uppercase accents while preserving their intended construction, alignment, and relationship to the uppercase forms they accompany.

### Controls

The uppercase acute accent is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTUA is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-accent child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
