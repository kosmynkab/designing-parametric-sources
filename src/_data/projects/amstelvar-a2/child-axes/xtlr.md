## Expected behaviour

XTLR changes horizontal transparency in lowercase round constructions while preserving their intended curve relationships and overall construction where possible.

### Controls

o and p provide round lowercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTLR is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-round child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
