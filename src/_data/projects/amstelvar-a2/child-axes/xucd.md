## Expected behaviour

XUCD changes the sidebearings of uppercase diagonal constructions while preserving their intended visual extent and relationship to neighbouring forms.

### Controls

A, K, and V provide diagonal uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XUCD is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-diagonal child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
