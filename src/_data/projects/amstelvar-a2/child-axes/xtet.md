## Expected behaviour

XTET changes horizontal transparency in punctuation and other glyphs while preserving each glyph’s intended construction and proportion where possible.

### Controls

The at sign is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTET is Amstelvar Avar2’s other-glyph child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
