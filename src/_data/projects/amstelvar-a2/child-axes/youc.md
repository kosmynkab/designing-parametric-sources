## Expected behaviour

YOUC changes the thickness of uppercase thin-stroke components while
preserving the intended construction and overall proportions of the glyph
where possible.

### Controls

H is the primary straight-stroke control. O tests how the same
transformation is carried through round construction.

### Notable construction cases

V tests how thin-stroke change is distributed through diagonal
construction and the point where the diagonals meet.

E tests the relationship between the thin horizontal arms and the
vertical stem.

### Evidence

The first selected comparisons appear on the
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) page. This document will later collect the
fuller uppercase evidence set, controls, and research notes for YOUC.

### Relationship to YOPQ

YOUC is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase implementation of the parent
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) thin-stroke transformation.
