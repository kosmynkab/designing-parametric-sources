## Expected behaviour

YSHF changes the height or depth of horizontal serifs in figures while preserving their intended construction, proportion, and spacing.

### Controls

One and four provide figure constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YSHA

YSHF is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [YSHA](/reference/axes/ysha/) horizontal-serif-height transformation.
