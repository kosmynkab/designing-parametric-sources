## Expected behaviour

YSVF changes the height or depth of vertical serifs in figures while preserving their intended construction, proportion, and spacing.

### Controls

Seven is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to YSVA

YSVF is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [YSVA](/reference/axes/ysva/) vertical-serif-height transformation.
