## Expected behaviour

XUCS changes the sidebearings of uppercase straight constructions while preserving the drawing and intended spacing relationship with neighbouring forms.

### Controls

H, E, and T provide straight-stem uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XUCS is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-straight child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
