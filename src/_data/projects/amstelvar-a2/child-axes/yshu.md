## Expected behaviour

YSHU changes the height or depth of horizontal serifs in uppercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, proportion, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

H, E, and V provide straight, multi-arm, and diagonal uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YSHA

YSHU is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase child axis of the parent [YSHA](/reference/axes/ysha/) horizontal-serif-height transformation.
