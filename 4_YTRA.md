YTRA
====

Expected behavior: character gets shorter/taller, while strokes and details “stay the same”.

Uppercase
---------

### Straight glyphs

- only YTRA changes
- only y-direction deltas

### Round glyphs

- internal curvature is controlled separately by XTEQ YTEQ parametric axes
- in Italic, xy-direction deltas are allowed for slanted & rotated shapes

### Diagonal glyphs

AmstelvarA2:
- xy-direction deltas allowed in the middle of slanted strokes and traps
- slanted measurements are aligned to stroke
- in Italic, XOPQ may change slightly (?)
