## Expected behaviour

XSHL changes the width of horizontal serifs in lowercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, continuity, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

n, r, and v provide arch, shoulder, and diagonal lowercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XSHA

XSHL is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase child axis of the parent [XSHA](/reference/axes/xsha/) horizontal-serif-width transformation.
