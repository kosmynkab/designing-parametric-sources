## Expected behaviour

XSHU changes the width of horizontal serifs in uppercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, proportion, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

H, E, and V provide straight, multi-arm, and diagonal uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XSHA

XSHU is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase child axis of the parent [XSHA](/reference/axes/xsha/) horizontal-serif-width transformation.
