## Expected behaviour

XOLA changes the thickness of thick-stroke components in lowercase accents
while preserving their intended construction, alignment, and relationship to
the lowercase forms they accompany.

### Controls

The lowercase acute accent is the current control glyph for this
transformation.

### Relationship to XOPQ

XOLA is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-accent child axis of the parent
[XOPQ](/reference/axes/xopq/) thick-stroke transformation.
