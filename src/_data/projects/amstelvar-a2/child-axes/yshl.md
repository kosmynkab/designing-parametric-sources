## Expected behaviour

YSHL changes the height or depth of horizontal serifs in lowercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, continuity, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

n, r, and v provide arch, shoulder, and diagonal lowercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YSHA

YSHL is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase child axis of the parent [YSHA](/reference/axes/ysha/) horizontal-serif-height transformation.
