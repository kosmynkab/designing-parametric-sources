## Expected behaviour

YSVL changes the height or depth of vertical serifs in lowercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, continuity, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

r is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to YSVA

YSVL is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase child axis of the parent [YSVA](/reference/axes/ysva/) vertical-serif-height transformation.
