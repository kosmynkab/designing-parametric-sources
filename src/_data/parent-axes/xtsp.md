## Expected behaviour

XTSP concerns side spacing. The intended transformation changes the space at
the left and right of a character while preserving the character’s internal
construction, proportion, and intended relationship to neighbouring forms.

> That transformation is different for different groups of characters – straight, round, diagonal might require separate child axes.
