## Expected behaviour

XSVU changes the horizontal extent of vertical serifs in uppercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, position in the skeletal construction, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

C, E, and T provide round, multi-arm, and straight uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XSVA

XSVU is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase child axis of the parent [XSVA](/reference/axes/xsva/) vertical-serif-width transformation.
