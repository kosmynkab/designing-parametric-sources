## Expected behaviour

XSVL changes the horizontal extent of vertical serifs in lowercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, continuity, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

r is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XSVA

XSVL is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase child axis of the parent [XSVA](/reference/axes/xsva/) vertical-serif-width transformation.
