## Expected behaviour

YTDE changes the vertical extent of lowercase descenders while preserving their intended relationship to the baseline and the rest of the glyph.

### Controls

p is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to YTRA

YTDE is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-descender child axis of the parent [YTRA](/reference/axes/ytra/) vertical-transparency transformation.
