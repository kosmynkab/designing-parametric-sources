## Expected behaviour

YTAS changes the vertical extent of lowercase ascenders while preserving their intended relationship to x-height and the rest of the glyph.

### Controls

l is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to YTRA

YTAS is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-ascender child axis of the parent [YTRA](/reference/axes/ytra/) vertical-transparency transformation.
