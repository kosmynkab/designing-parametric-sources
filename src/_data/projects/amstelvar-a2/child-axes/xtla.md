## Expected behaviour

XTLA changes horizontal transparency in lowercase accents while preserving their intended construction, alignment, and relationship to the lowercase forms they accompany.

### Controls

The lowercase acute accent is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTLA is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-accent child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
