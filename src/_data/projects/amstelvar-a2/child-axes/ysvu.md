## Expected behaviour

YSVU changes the height or depth of vertical serifs in uppercase glyphs while preserving their intended attachment, position in the skeletal construction, and relationship to the stem.

### Controls

C, E, and T provide round, multi-arm, and straight uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YSVA

YSVU is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase child axis of the parent [YSVA](/reference/axes/ysva/) vertical-serif-height transformation.
