## Expected behaviour

YTUC changes vertical transparency in uppercase glyphs while preserving their intended horizontal structure, stroke relationships, and overall construction where possible.

### Controls

H, O, and V provide straight, round, and diagonal uppercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YTRA

YTUC is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase child axis of the parent [YTRA](/reference/axes/ytra/) vertical-transparency transformation.
