## Expected behaviour

YOUA changes the thickness of thin-stroke components in uppercase accents
while preserving their intended construction, alignment, and relationship to
the uppercase forms they accompany.

### Controls

The uppercase acute accent is the current control glyph for this
transformation.

### Relationship to YOPQ

YOUA is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-accent child axis of the parent
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) thin-stroke transformation.
