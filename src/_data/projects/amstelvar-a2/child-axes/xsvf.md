## Expected behaviour

XSVF changes the horizontal extent of vertical serifs in figures while preserving their intended construction, proportion, and spacing.

### Controls

Seven is the current control glyph for this transformation.

### Relationship to XSVA

XSVF is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [XSVA](/reference/axes/xsva/) vertical-serif-width transformation.
