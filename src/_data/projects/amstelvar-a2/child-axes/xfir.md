## Expected behaviour

XFIR changes the sidebearings of figures while preserving their intended visual extent, proportions, and relationship to neighbouring numerals.

### Controls

Zero, one, and seven provide round, straight-stem, and diagonal figure constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XFIR is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
