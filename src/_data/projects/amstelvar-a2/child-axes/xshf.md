## Expected behaviour

XSHF changes the width of horizontal serifs in figures while preserving their intended construction, proportion, and spacing.

### Controls

One and four provide figure constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XSHA

XSHF is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [XSHA](/reference/axes/xsha/) horizontal-serif-width transformation.
