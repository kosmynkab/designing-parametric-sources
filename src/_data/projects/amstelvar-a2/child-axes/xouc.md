## Expected behaviour

XOUC changes the thickness of uppercase thick-stroke components while
preserving the intended construction and overall proportions of the glyph
where possible.

### Controls

H is the primary straight-stroke control. O tests how the same
transformation is carried through round construction.

### Notable construction cases

V requires diagonal delta adjustments, relating the thick-stroke
transformation to changing weight along the diagonal construction.

E tests the relationship between a single vertical stem and several
horizontal arms, including its relationship with
[XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/).

### Evidence

The first selected comparisons appear on the
[XOPQ](/reference/axes/xopq/) page. This document will later collect the
fuller uppercase evidence set, controls, and research notes for XOUC.

### Relationship to XOPQ

XOUC is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase implementation of the parent
[XOPQ](/reference/axes/xopq/) thick-stroke transformation.
