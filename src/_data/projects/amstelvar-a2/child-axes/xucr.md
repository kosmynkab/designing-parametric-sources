## Expected behaviour

XUCR changes the sidebearings of uppercase round constructions while preserving their intended visual extent and relationship to neighbouring forms.

### Controls

C, O, and P provide uppercase round constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XUCR is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-round child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
