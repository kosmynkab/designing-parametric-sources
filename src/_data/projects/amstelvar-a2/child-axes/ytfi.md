## Expected behaviour

YTFI changes vertical transparency in figures while preserving their intended construction, proportions, and spacing where possible.

### Controls

Zero, one, and seven provide round, straight-stem, and diagonal figure constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YTRA

YTFI is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent [YTRA](/reference/axes/ytra/) vertical-transparency transformation.
