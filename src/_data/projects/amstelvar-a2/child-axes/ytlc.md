## Expected behaviour

YTLC changes vertical transparency at lowercase x-height while preserving the intended structure and relationships of lowercase forms where possible.

### Controls

n, o, p, and v provide straight, round, descender, and diagonal lowercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to YTRA

YTLC is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-x-height child axis of the parent [YTRA](/reference/axes/ytra/) vertical-transparency transformation.
