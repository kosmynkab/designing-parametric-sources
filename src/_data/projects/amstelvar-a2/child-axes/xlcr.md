## Expected behaviour

XLCR changes the sidebearings of lowercase round constructions while preserving their intended visual extent and relationship to neighbouring forms.

### Controls

o and p provide round lowercase constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTSP

XLCR is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-round child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
