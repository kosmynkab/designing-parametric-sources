## Expected behaviour

YOFI changes the thickness of thin-stroke components in figures while
preserving their intended construction, proportions, and spacing where
possible.

### Controls

Zero, one, four, and seven provide complementary figure constructions for
assessing the transformation: round, straight-stem, open, and diagonal forms.

### Relationship to YOPQ

YOFI is Amstelvar Avar2’s figure-specific child axis of the parent
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) thin-stroke transformation.
