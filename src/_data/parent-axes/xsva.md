## Expected behaviour

XSVA concerns the horizontal scale of vertical serifs. The intended
transformation changes their thickness while preserving their attachment,
proportion, and relationship to the vertical construction they complete.

> serifs should retain their position in the skeletal construction of the character - that is assessed optically.
