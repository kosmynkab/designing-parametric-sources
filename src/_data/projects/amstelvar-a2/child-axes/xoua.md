## Expected behaviour

XOUA changes the thickness of thick-stroke components in uppercase accents
while preserving their intended construction, alignment, and relationship to
the uppercase forms they accompany.

### Controls

The uppercase acute accent is the current control glyph for this
transformation.

### Relationship to XOPQ

XOUA is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-accent child axis of the parent
[XOPQ](/reference/axes/xopq/) thick-stroke transformation.
