## Expected behaviour

YOET changes the thickness of thin-stroke components in punctuation and other
glyphs while preserving each glyph’s intended construction and proportion
where possible.

### Controls

Exclamation mark, parenthesis, and at sign provide different constructions for
assessing the transformation across punctuation and symbols.

### Relationship to YOPQ

YOET is Amstelvar Avar2’s other-glyph child axis of the parent
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) thin-stroke transformation.
