## Expected behaviour

YOLA changes the thickness of thin-stroke components in lowercase accents
while preserving their intended construction, alignment, and relationship to
the lowercase forms they accompany.

### Controls

The lowercase acute accent is the current control glyph for this
transformation.

### Relationship to YOPQ

YOLA is Amstelvar Avar2’s lowercase-accent child axis of the parent
[YOPQ](/reference/axes/yopq/) thin-stroke transformation.
