## Expected behaviour

XTUC changes horizontal transparency in uppercase straight constructions while preserving their intended vertical structure, stroke relationships, and overall construction where possible.

### Controls

H, E, and T provide straight-stem constructions for assessing the transformation.

### Relationship to XTRA

XTUC is Amstelvar Avar2’s uppercase-straight child axis of the parent [XTRA](/reference/axes/xtra/) horizontal-transparency transformation.
