## Expected behaviour

XETS changes the sidebearings of punctuation and other glyphs while preserving each glyph’s intended visual extent and relationship to neighbouring forms.

### Controls

Exclamation mark, parenthesis, and at sign provide different constructions for assessing the transformation across punctuation and symbols.

### Relationship to XTSP

XETS is Amstelvar Avar2’s other-glyph child axis of the parent [XTSP](/reference/axes/xtsp/) side-spacing transformation.
